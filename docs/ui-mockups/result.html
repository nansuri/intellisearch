How do jastip services work?

AI overview

Jastip (jasa titip, or "buy-for-me") is a service where a trusted person purchases items on your behalf — usually from a store or market you cannot visit yourself, often abroad.[1][2]

The typical flow is: you agree on the item and fee, the buyer purchases and ships it, and you pay after delivery or via a deposit.[3] Most arrangements are informal and built on trust and local reputation, though platforms now add escrow-style protections.[4][5]

In short: jastip = personal shopping as a service, popular for imported goods, limited-edition items, and food delivery.

How much do jastip services typically charge?

Fees vary widely — from a flat 5–10% commission on the item price to a fixed per-kilo rate for food.[2] High-value or bulky items usually add a deposit to cover risk.[3]
